How does Ambient Profile Affect the Taste of Fresh Food in Southeast Asia? Southeast Asia is a major trading region for not only the Asia Pacific Region but also serves as the bridge between America and Europe. When food travels hundreds of miles to its final table it is important to ensure its flavor freshness is maintained till the last mile.
